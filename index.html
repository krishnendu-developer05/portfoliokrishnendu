MuleShield

Details [+]

Graph-based financial forensic engine analyzing account relationships, circular transaction cycles, and rapid pass-through velocities.

Python / TS Graph DFS C++ Kernels
Live Prototype ↗
Problem & Solution

Criminal syndicates disperse stolen funds across fast-payment networks through chains of mule accounts. MuleShield uses graph topology algorithms (Tarjan SCC, DFS) to isolate high-risk money-mule rings.

  • Multi-hop graph cycle traversal and circular layering heuristics.
  • Rapid pass-through fund velocity and smurfing detection.
  • Forensic investigator dashboard with risk-gated intervention concept.

TypeScript Agentic Loop Market APIs

Problem & Solution

Traders struggle to calculate cross-asset correlation risks and portfolio drawdown exposure during volatility spikes. StockRiskAgent decomposes risk queries, calls market tools, and produces structured risk reports.
